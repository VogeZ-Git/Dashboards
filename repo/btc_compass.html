Filtered out deliberately ignored — most still live on the full dashboard

Exchange balances & flowsProprietary, frequently re-labelled wallets; the default reading (inflow = selling) is often backwards. Check: bin it.
Whale / shrimp cohort balancesExchange and custodian wallets contaminate the size buckets — you can't tell a whale buying from an exchange consolidating.
Fixed horizontal cycle levels"Must hit realised price / MVRV 0.8 / Pi-cycle cross" — dormant coins (Satoshi et al.) bias these; peaks and troughs keep shrinking. Replaced by rolling-4y percentiles and momentum.
Pi-cycle, Investor Tool, Euphoria Zone & other fixed top checklistsThe classic checklist didn't fire at the 2025 top (Pi-cycle never crossed; MVRV never reached its old bands). The idea that works — distance from many anchors — lives in the Cycle Heat Index instead.
Hash ribbons, Puell & miner modelsAI/HPC conversions pull hashrate for reasons unrelated to BTC price; ribbons have been printing repeatedly and no longer mark capitulation. Miners are structurally ~50/50 profitable by design.
Stock-to-flow, terminal / balanced / delta priceMoon-math or throwaway models. Even their authors don't use them for levels.
Full-history SOPR and vanilla MVRV levelsDrift as the holder base ages. STH-SOPR / STH-MVRV (fixed 155-day window) keep the same meaning over time.
Power law as a targetStatistically real, not predictive. Shown for context only.
The four-year calendarNo mechanism. This cycle's capitulations came in Feb and Jun 2026, not October. Read behaviour, then check the date.
Address counts, NVT, velocity, feesMuch of the demand now sits in ETFs and custodians off-chain, so raw on-chain activity says little about it (judgement call, not Check's).

How to read this

Each signal is scored +1 / 0 / −1 by the rule shown when you open its row; a horizon's score is the plain average of its scored signals. The rules are transparent heuristics, not a fitted model — read the levels and the direction of change, not the decimal. "4y%" = where today's value sits within the last four years (Check's percentile framing: "how many days were worse than this?"). Curation follows James Check's Oct-2026 read: weight human behaviour (realised P/L, STH cost basis, capitulation) over fixed thresholds; use rates of change; ignore exchange flows and the calendar.
