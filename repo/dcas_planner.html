DCAS Planner

You read the multiplier off the chart. This does the money: base-amount sizing, the cash bank that funds the >1× buys, the shadow-DCA comparison, and the one statistic that says whether the model is earning its keep — cov(multiplier, price).

What this is not It does not compute or approximate the DCAS multiplier — that lives in a closed TradingView indicator and nothing here reconstructs it. Every multiplier is a number you typed in after reading the chart. See the field manual §07 for what the model does not disclose.

Settings

The bank yield is credited on the time-weighted bank balance between logged periods, so the DCAS-vs-DCA comparison charges DCA for having deployed capital earlier. Set it to your actual cash rate, or 0 to ignore it.

Data

Everything lives in this browser's local storage for this page's address only. It does not sync between your PC and laptop, and it is not backed up — export the JSON into L:\Sync 2\Investor\Trading Brain\DCAS\ whenever you have logged something you would not want to retype.
