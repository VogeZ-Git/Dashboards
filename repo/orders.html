How this works

Prices come from the same Apps Script feed as the scanner pages, every 2 minutes; without it the page falls back to the last scanner close and says so. Triggered = the live price is through the limit now. Touched = a daily or 4-hour open or close since the order was placed went through the limit (from the scanner's price caches) — it has probably filled. Intraday wicks are not in those caches, so a fill on a wick shows only as a near miss: always confirm in the broker. Distances for EPA:REMX are measured on the US REMX listing against the US-equivalent level. Marking orders: unlock with your order key (the sheet badge), open a row and press Filled / Cancelled / Placed / Reopen. The change is written to the Order Book Google Sheet at once (Status + Log tabs) and shows on every device; a fill releases the reserve orders tied to it. The daily run syncs the sheet back into orders.json. Without the key, the copy buttons give the same command for python orders_build.py in Python\OrderBook.
