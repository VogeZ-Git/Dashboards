1. The mail — your daily step

Takes under two minutes. Most days it takes ten seconds.

  1. Read the subject line only. It looks like [signals] 1 ACT · 15 regime — APLD.
  2. If it says 0 ACT or "nothing new" — close it. You are done. No mail at all also means nothing to do.
  3. If it says N ACT, open it and read only the green cards at the top. Those are the ones with a decision in them.
  4. Skip the "Watch" and "Regime changes" tables during the week. They are there for Saturday.
  5. Decide act or skip using the rules below. Then close the mail.

If you see a red STALE FEED banner

The scanner has not published recently, so every state in the mail is from an older close. Ignore the whole mail and check that the PC ran confluence.bat.

What the lines on a green card mean

LineRead it as
zone (weekly)Is the slow timeframe pointing up. LONG is with you, FLAT is against. Extra information only — it never blocks a buy on its own.
regime (daily)This is the one that decides. Must say LONG. Also tells you how many days since it flipped.
levelWhere price is on the ATR ladder. "entry from underneath" is good. "pushing into resistance" means you are paying up.
edgeTwo different measurements of the model on that ticker. If the card flags THESE DISAGREE, treat the model as unproven on that name and decide on the thesis instead.
bookWhat you already own in it, or "not held — candidate".
callYour own standing call from that ticker's Synopsis.md. If it says there is no Synopsis, you have no written thesis behind this line.
bucketHow often this kind of signal has actually worked. A THIN warning means too few samples to trust the number.

Act or skip

Act when all four are true

  • regime (daily) says LONG
  • level says entry from underneath, or at least not "into resistance"
  • the call does not forbid adding, and the position is not already at its ceiling
  • you have a thesis — a Synopsis exists, or you are happy to open a small starter line

First layer is 0.25–0.5% of the book. You are not trying to fill a position in one go.

Skip — no thinking required

  • "pushing into resistance" — wait for a pullback
  • "ladder void" or "proxy row" — the price levels on that ticker are not reliable, so there is no level to buy at
  • "no Synopsis behind this line" and it is not held — that is a research task, not a trade
  • edges disagree — information, not an instruction
  • anything in WATCH or REST

A sell signal will never appear in this mail. Sells were measured to have no edge, so they are filtered out. If you want to exit, that is a Saturday decision from the Monitor and the Synopsis — not a signal.

2. Saturday — the review about 30 minutes

  1. Open the Weekly Asset Review in L:\Sync 2\Investor\Orders\ — newest date. It was generated overnight and opens with a scorecard against last week's calls.
  2. Open the Task list beside it. Work top-down: urgent, then short term. Leave background alone.
  3. Open signals.html → SIGNALS tab. Skim what fired this week. You are looking for patterns, not entries.
  4. Open overseer.html → ACTIONS tab. Click the WEEKLY CONFIRMS filter. That is your shortlist: names where the weekly and daily both point up.
  5. Open the Monitor for the per-asset chapters — prices in play, your conditions, the ATR rails.
  6. Write the orders down. Ticker, limit price, size. Do not place them yet.

Two honesty rules on the overseer page

A ▼ count means "stretched, do not buy here" — it is not a sell signal. And agreement is not edge: nothing says 5/5 beats 2/5. The AGREE column ranks a shortlist, it does not predict.

3. Sunday — place the orders about 20 minutes

  1. Open the order list you wrote on Saturday.
  2. Place the limits at Saxo and DEGIRO.
  3. Check free margin before the last one, not after.
  4. Done for the week. Monday needs nothing from you.

4. Monthly about an hour, first Sunday

  1. Run optimize_monthly.bat.
  2. Run python build_pine_alerts.py afterwards so the TradingView alert matches the new presets.
  3. Write or refresh two or three Synopsis.md files — start with held lines that have none.
  4. Close three items off the task list.

5. Quarterly half a day

  1. Re-underwrite the big positions against the earnings diary.
  2. Check every held line still has a case folder and a Synopsis.
  3. Decide the cash level for the next quarter.

If something looks wrong

SymptomDo this
No mail for several daysNormal if nothing fired. A Saturday heartbeat mail always arrives — if that is missing too, the scanner or the script is down.
Red STALE FEED bannerThe PC has not run confluence.bat. Run it.
"feed unreachable" mailThe published feed is missing. Check the push log, then re-run confluence.bat. Only one such mail a day will arrive.
"Verdict layer failed" bannerThe scanner is fine but the decision context is missing. The signals are still real; treat them as bare rows until it is fixed.
A price looks obviously wrongCheck whether the line is tagged proxy or ladder void — those are deliberately unreliable and the card says so.
Want the current feed re-sentIn the Apps Script editor run digestResetMemory, then digestTest.
