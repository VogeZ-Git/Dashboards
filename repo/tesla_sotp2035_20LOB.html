Price is then cap ÷ projected shares, where shares grow from today's ~3.76B by the dilution slider over 9 years to 2035. James's headline targets ($2,681 / $5,181 / $8,188) only hold at ~3.5B shares — which is below today's count, i.e. they quietly bake in ~0.8%/yr of net buybacks. Flip the slider to a realistic +1%/yr and you reach ~4.1B shares and every price falls ~15%; Musk's pending comp package pushes dilution higher still, not lower. Even the bear case is ~6× today's price and prices in a working robotaxi + Optimus business — a true bear (autonomy stalls, EV margins compress) sits below today, and no preset here represents that.
